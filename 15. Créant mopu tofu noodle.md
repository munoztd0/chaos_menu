Ingredients
600g thick udon noodles

4 tbsp peanut puree

200g silken tofu

60ml heavy cream

3 tbsp gochujang

2.5 tbsp soy sauce

1 garlic cloves, peeled

1 tbsp fresh ginger, peeled

1 tbsp toasted sesame oil

1 tsp sugar

1/2 tsp ground Sichuan peppercorn

100g extra-firm tofu

2 spring onions (green parts), sliced

3 tbsp neutral oil (divided)

1 tsp gochugaru (or sweet paprika)

Pinch of salt

Instructions
Red Oil: Warm 2 tbsp neutral oil. Pour over gochugaru. Let steep.

Noodles: Boil noodles until cooked. Drain.

Crispy Tofu: Crumble extra-firm tofu. Fry in a pan with 1 tbsp neutral oil and salt until golden and crispy. Remove from pan.

Sauce: Blend silken tofu, cream, gochujang, soy sauce, garlic, ginger, sesame oil, sugar, and Sichuan peppercorn until smooth.

Combine: Heat the blended sauce in the pan. Add noodles. Toss until warm and coated.

Serve: Plate noodles. Top with crispy tofu, a drizzle of the steeped red oil, and spring onions.

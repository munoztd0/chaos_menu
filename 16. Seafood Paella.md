10–12 people

Ingredients: 1–1.2 kg rice · 3.5–4 L fish stock · 1 kg mussels · 700–800 g clams · 700–1000 g prawns/shrimp · 700–800 g squid/cuttlefish · 2 peppers · 8 garlic cloves · 400–500 g tomato · saffron · sweet paprika · olive oil · salt.

1. Sofrito

Oil → squid 5–7 min, remove.
Pepper 8–10 min → garlic → tomato 10–15 min until very concentrated → paprika + saffron 10 sec.

2. Stock

Add 3.5–4 L hot stock. It should cover the pan by about 1.5–2 cm. Taste: slightly saltier than you want the final dish. Boil 5 min.

3. Rice

Add rice and spread evenly in a thin layer (~1–1.5 cm).
Do not stir again.
High heat 8–10 min → medium heat.

4. Seafood

Minute 8–10: clams.

Minute 12–15: raw prawns/shrimp + squid if you removed it.

Last 3–5 min: already-cooked mussels.


5. Finish

At 15 min, start tasting the rice every 2–3 min.

It should be tender but slightly firm in the centre.

Rice hard + pan dry → add a little hot stock.

Rice almost done + too much liquid → increase heat.


When almost dry → high heat 30–60 sec for socarrat. If it smells burnt, stop immediately.

6. Rest

5–10 min off the heat. Serve with lemon.

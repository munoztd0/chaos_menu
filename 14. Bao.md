

Dough

2½ cups (315 g) all-purpose flour

2½ tsp baking powder

2 tbsp sugar

½ tsp salt

2 tbsp neutral oil

¾ cup (180 ml) warm water (add gradually)


Filling

75–100 g dry textured soy protein (TVP)

1½ cups boiling water or vegetable broth

2 cups finely shredded cabbage

2 cloves garlic, minced

1 tsp grated fresh ginger

2 tbsp neutral oil

3 tbsp hoisin sauce

1 tbsp soy sauce

1–2 tbsp sriracha (to taste)

1 tsp sesame oil (optional)

1 tsp cornstarch mixed with 1 tbsp water (optional, if the filling is too wet)

Sliced spring onion (optional)


Make the filling

1. Soak the TVP in the boiling water or broth for 10 minutes.


2. Drain well and squeeze out as much liquid as possible.


3. Heat the oil in a pan. Cook the garlic and ginger for 30 seconds.


4. Add the TVP and cook for 3–4 minutes until lightly browned.


5. Add the cabbage and cook until softened, about 4–5 minutes.


6. Stir in the hoisin, soy sauce, sriracha, and sesame oil.


7. If the mixture is still wet, stir in the cornstarch slurry and cook for 1 minute.


8. Let the filling cool completely before assembling.



Make the dough

1. Mix the flour, baking powder, sugar, and salt.


2. Add the oil and most of the warm water.


3. Mix into a soft dough, adding a little more water if needed.


4. Knead for 5–8 minutes until smooth.


5. Cover and let it rest for 15 minutes.



Assemble

1. Divide the dough into 8 equal pieces.


2. Roll each into a 10–12 cm circle, leaving the center slightly thicker.


3. Place 1–2 tablespoons of cooled filling in the center.


4. Pleat and pinch the top closed.


5. Place each bun on a square of baking paper.


6. Let them rest for another 10–15 minutes.



Steam

1. Bring water to a boil in a steamer.


2. Steam the bao for 12–15 minutes.


3. Turn off the heat and leave the lid closed for 2–3 minutes before opening.
